How to play

Three lanes each have a shared card in the middle. Play cards from your hand into your side of a lane, four per side. Your four cards plus the shared card make a poker hand, and the better hand takes the lane. Win two lanes to win the battle.

Aces are always high, and there is no A-2-3-4-5 straight. The Tutorial on the title screen walks through the board in eight steps.

Common questions

Why did a lane close before it was full?
A lane closes as soon as the trailing side can no longer win it, even with perfect draws.
Who wins a tie?
Whoever completed their side of the lane first.
Is my voyage saved?
Yes. A game in progress is saved on your iPhone and offered when you reopen the app.
How do I reset everything?
Delete and reinstall the app. All saved data lives on your device, so this removes it completely.

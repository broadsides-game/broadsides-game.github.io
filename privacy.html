Privacy Policy

Effective 3 November 2026.

What we collect

Nothing. Broadsides: Naval Poker does not collect, store or share any personal information. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no in-app purchases, and it sends nothing to us or to anyone else.

What stays on your device

Your settings and any game in progress are saved on your iPhone so you can pick up where you left off. That data never leaves your device, and deleting the app deletes it.

Children

Because the app collects no information from anyone, it collects none from children.

Changes

If this ever changes, this page will be updated before the app is, and the effective date above will change.
